# Proprietary evals are competitive moats

**Claim**: Public benchmarks are largely marketing, while proprietary evals represent genuine competitive moats for AI companies.

**Why it matters**: When everyone uses the same public benchmarks (MMLU, HumanEval, etc.), those benchmarks stop differentiating anyone. Internal eval pipelines that capture domain-specific quality signals are what actually separate top performers.

**Key insight**: Companies should invest heavily in building proprietary eval datasets and pipelines that reflect their specific use cases, rather than relying on public leaderboard performance.

**Framework for proprietary evals**:
1. Canonical queries - representative samples from real production traffic
2. End-to-end evaluations - test full pipeline, not isolated components
3. Date reasoning and freshness - eval models on temporal reasoning
4. Custom dataset construction - domain-specific test cases
