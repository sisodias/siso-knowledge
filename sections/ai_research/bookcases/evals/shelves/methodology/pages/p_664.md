# Vibes are expensive but accurate eval scoring functions

**Claim**: "Vibes" - subjective human assessment - are actually expensive but extraordinarily accurate scoring functions for AI evaluation.

**Why it matters**: While automated metrics are cheap and fast, they often miss what matters to users. Human "vibe checks" remain the gold standard, though expensive at scale.

**Key insight**: The goal is building cheaper proxies for vibes - finding structured signals that approximate human judgment without the cost of human evaluation on every change.

**Techniques**:
- A/B testing with human raters as the ultimate signal
- Small panel of expert evaluators for high-stakes decisions
- Gradual reduction of human evaluation as automated proxies mature
