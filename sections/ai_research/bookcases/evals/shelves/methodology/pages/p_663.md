# Evals as first derivative signals

**Claim**: Evals enable velocity by serving as "first derivative" signals - knowing whether a change improves things before shipping.

**Why it matters**: First derivative signals let teams iterate faster by providing immediate feedback on whether model changes, prompt updates, or system modifications actually improve outcomes. Without evals, teams fly blind.

**Key insight**: Evals should measure the *rate of change* in quality, not just absolute quality scores. This means building eval suites that trigger on regressions before they reach production.
