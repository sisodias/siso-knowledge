# VRoPE - Rotary Position Embedding for Video LLMs

**Repository**: [CASIA-IVA-Lab/VRoPE](https://github.com/CASIA-IVA-Lab/VRoPE)

**Stars**: 27 | **Language**: Python | **Updated**: 2025-12-31

**Description**: EMNLP 2025 Main paper. Official implementation of VRoPE: Rotary Position Embedding for Video Large Language Models. Handles temporal dimension in video understanding.

**Research**: Extends RoPE to handle the temporal dimension in video LLM tasks. Critical for video understanding at long context lengths where temporal ordering matters.

**Why it matters**: Video LLMs require different position encoding strategies than text. VRoPE provides a principled approach for temporal reasoning in long video contexts.
