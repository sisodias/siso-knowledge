# CLIP embeddings enable cross-modal image-text similarity at scale

**Claim**: CLIP (OpenAI) and its derivatives (2.7k stars for clip-retrieval) provide zero-shot image-text matching by projecting both modalities into a shared embedding space, enabling text-to-image and image-to-text retrieval.

**Why it matters**: CLIP embeddings are the foundation of most multimodal RAG systems, allowing text queries to retrieve relevant images without labeled image-caption pairs.
