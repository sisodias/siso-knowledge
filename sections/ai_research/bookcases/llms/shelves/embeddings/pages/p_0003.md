# MTEB benchmark standardizes evaluation across 58 text embedding tasks

**Claim**: Massive Text Embedding Benchmark (MTEB, 3.2k stars) covers 58 datasets across 112 languages for retrieval, clustering, classification, and pair classification tasks, becoming the de facto standard for comparing embedding models.

**Why it matters**: Without MTEB, comparing BGE vs Sentence-BERT vs E5 was apples-to-oranges. MTEB gave the community a shared leaderboard, driving rapid improvement in retrieval-oriented embeddings.

**Source**: https://github.com/embeddings-benchmark/mteb
