# Canonical corpus

`sections/` is the authored source of atomic Knowledge pages and hierarchy metadata.

- Follow `../module_templates/`; do not invent a second schema.
- One page owns one insight. Preserve source, creator, rights, score, tier, and provenance.
- Private/client material and unknown-rights payloads do not enter the public corpus.
- `_index/` and `graph/` are projections; never edit them as a substitute for changing a page.
- Use the deterministic add/query/rebuild commands and verify before publication.
