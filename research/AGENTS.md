# SISO Knowledge Research Works

This folder holds independent repositories whose durable outcome is research knowledge.
They are direct Works, not submodules of the parent Knowledge repository.

- `siso-foundry` acquires and ranks traceable research knowledge at corpus scale.
- `siso-evidence-engines` turns explicit sources into grounded knowledge, principles, and ideas.
- Read each repository's own `AGENTS.md` before changing it.
- Preserve independent Git histories and local dirty state.
- Do not refile a Research Work under `SISO_Agents` merely because agents execute it.
- Large corpora, databases, transcripts, logs, credentials, and generated state stay outside Git.

Human map: `README.html`.
