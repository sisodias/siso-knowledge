Research Works

Independent, forkable software whose durable outcome is traceable research knowledge. Agents may operate these systems, but operation does not determine ownership.

SISO Foundry

Acquisition, identity, provenance, ranking, and reusable knowledge over large external corpora. Source stays in Git; large data lives in its external data plane.

SISO Evidence Engines

Deterministic, evidence-gated engines that convert explicit sources into grounded knowledge items, principles, and ranked improvement ideas.

Boundary

These repositories are flat independent Works under the Knowledge domain. They do not belong inside the agent runtime, Skills Hub, or Playbook. Their Git histories and release cycles remain independent from the parent Knowledge checkout.
